# Finance Control Plane

Modern finance teams need more than dashboards. They need a control plane that combines three layers:

- Quantitative truth: warehouse-backed models, metrics, reconciliations, and close/reporting tables.
- Qualitative context: policies, decisions, account knowledge, business changes, and operating notes.
- Agent harnesses: repeatable workflows that can retrieve context, run checks, draft analysis, and leave an audit trail.

The opportunity is not to replace finance judgment. It is to make the judgment loop faster, more legible, and less dependent on scattered memory.

## Why it matters

Most finance teams already have fragments of this system. The problem is that the fragments live in different places: spreadsheets, dashboards, Slack threads, docs, tickets, and people's heads.

A useful AI finance system has to connect those fragments without hiding the reasoning.
