# Projects

## Databricks / Plaid personal finance system

A personal finance pipeline with Plaid ingestion, Databricks-backed modeling, dashboards, and weekly review automation.

The interesting part is not the app surface. It is the operating pattern: raw transactions become modeled tables, the tables become review surfaces, and the review surfaces become a recurring financial workflow.

## Obsidian + Codex operating system

A local knowledge and automation setup that turns daily notes, tasks, research, prompts, and recurring reviews into a lightweight personal operating system.
